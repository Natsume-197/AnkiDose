**AnkiDose**

Defaults for the *Tools → AnkiDose → New plan…* dialog (they update automatically to whatever you chose last).

- `hours`: how many hours you want to finish in.
- `block_minutes`: how often a new block opens.
- `behind_mode`: what happens to the cards you didn't do in a block.
  - `"spread"`: they're spread across all the remaining blocks.
  - `"carry"`: they're added to the next block.
- `count_new`: `true` to also count new cards (not just reviews).
- `block_end_notice`: `"dialog"` (a window with *Keep reviewing* / *Take a break*) or `"tooltip"` (a small message).
- `system_notifications`: system notification when a block opens or ends.
- `sound`: beep when a block opens or ends.

Reviews done in AnkiDroid / AnkiMobile are counted when you sync on desktop.
